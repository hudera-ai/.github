## How we work

1. **Discovery call**: free, 30 minutes, on one process that costs your team the most time.
2. **Clickable prototype**: a demo built on your workflow, for your team to try and correct.
3. **Pilot & launch**: connected to your WhatsApp, files and systems for one team.
4. **Run & improve**: monthly operation, monitoring and support with clear limits.
